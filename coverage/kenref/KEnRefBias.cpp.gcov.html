      71             : Restrains an ensemble of replicas against NMR observables (kinetic ensemble refinement).

      73             : KENREF computes an NMR observable as an average over the whole ensemble of replicas, compares it with
      74             : experimental data, and applies the resulting restraint force to the atoms. Unlike most biases it acts on
      75             : coordinates rather than on collective variables, so it takes **no** `ARG`.
      76             : 
      77             : The energy model is selected by name with `MODEL`:
      78             : 
      79             : | `MODEL`    | restrains                                          |
      80             : |:-----------|:---------------------------------------------------|
      81             : | `SIGMA`    | cross-relaxation rates (interproton NOE build-up)  |
      82             : | `PLATEAUS` | NOE plateau values                                 |
      83             : | `RELAX`    | longitudinal / transverse relaxation               |
      84             : 
      85             : Because the observable is an ensemble average, `RELAX` needs at least two replicas (run with `--multi`);
      86             : `SIGMA` and `PLATEAUS` also work with a single replica.
      87             :

      89             : that maps atom names to indices, and is also used as the reference structure when `REF` is omitted. When

      92             : clamps the resulting force to `MAX_FORCE`.
